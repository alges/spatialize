# Facies, porosity, permeability and acoustic impedance (GeoDataSets, M. J. Pyrcz)

Two synthetic 2D well data sets of a reservoir with two facies (0 shale, 1 sand), from the GeoDataSets
repository of Michael J. Pyrcz (https://github.com/GeostatsGuy/GeoDataSets):

- `spatial_nonlinear_MV_facies_v5.csv`: 450 wells over 10 km × 10 km (`Well`, `X`, `Y` in m, `Por`
  porosity %, `Perm` permeability mD, `AI` acoustic impedance, `Facies`).
- `sample_data_MV_biased.csv`: 368 wells over 1 km × 1 km sampled preferentially (`X`, `Y` in m,
  `Facies`, `Porosity` fraction, `Perm` mD, `AI`).
