.. _partitions:

**********
Partitions
**********

The encoder's random partitions on their own: which locations share a cell, the co-occurrence of
sets of locations and the law of the groupings of a few locations, with the closed forms of the
theory's Mondrian process to compare with (:doc:`../theory/encoders`). With the same ``p_process``,
``alpha``, ``n_partitions`` and ``seed``, these functions see the partitions an estimation draws.
